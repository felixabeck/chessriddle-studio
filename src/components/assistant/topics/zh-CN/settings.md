从应用程序菜单打开 [[page:settings]] ({{label:SideBar.Settings}})（或在 macOS 上按 Cmd+,）。使用 [[page:settings]] 顶部的 {{label:Common.Search}} 栏按标题、描述或关键字筛选所有设置，按 Escape 清除搜索。

设置分为九个类别：
- [[settings-category:board]] ({{label:Settings.Board}})：棋盘行为与布局，包括 [[setting:piece-dest]]、[[setting:move-highlight]]、[[setting:move-highlight-color]]、[[setting:move-highlight-opacity]]、[[setting:arrows]]、[[setting:variation-arrows]]、[[setting:move-notation]]、[[setting:move-method]]、[[setting:snap-arrows]]、[[setting:consecutive-arrows]]、[[setting:erase-drawables]]、[[setting:auto-promote]]、[[setting:coordinates]]、[[setting:animation-speed]]、[[setting:auto-save]]、[[setting:preview-board]]、[[setting:flip-board-after-move]]、[[setting:scroll-moves]]、[[setting:choose-variation]]、[[setting:material-display]]、[[setting:default-panel-tab]] 和 [[setting:panels-beside-notation]]。
- [[settings-category:inputs]] ({{label:Settings.Inputs}})：通过 [[setting:text-input]] 和 [[setting:spell-check]] 进行文本走棋输入和评注拼写检查。
- [[settings-category:appearance]] ({{label:Settings.Appearance}})：通过 [[setting:theme]]、[[setting:language]]、{{label:Settings.Appearance.TitleBar}}（Windows 和 Linux）、[[setting:font-size]]、[[setting:piece-set]]、[[setting:piece-size]]、[[setting:board-image]] 和 [[setting:board-wood-texture]] 进行视觉自定义。
- [[settings-category:sound]] ({{label:Settings.Sound}})：通过 [[setting:volume]] 和 [[setting:sound-collection]] 调整走棋音量和音效包。
- [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}})：自定义键盘快捷键、通过 {{label:Common.Reset}} 重置快捷键，并查看 {{label:Settings.Drawing}} 鼠标修饰键。
- [[settings-category:directories]] ({{label:Settings.Directories}})：通过 [[setting:files-directory]]、[[setting:databases-directory]]、[[setting:engines-directory]] 和 [[setting:puzzles-directory]] 配置存储文件夹。
- [[settings-category:repertoire]] ({{label:Settings.Repertoire}})：通过 [[setting:repertoire-depth]]、[[setting:practice-grading]] 和 [[setting:practice-new-per-session]] 设置开局库覆盖阈值与练习安排。
- [[settings-category:coach]] ({{label:Settings.AI}})：管理 Coach 和 Assistant 的 AI 连接，并在 [[setting:coach-engine]] 中选择 Coach 使用的本地引擎。
- [[settings-category:advanced]] ({{label:Settings.Advanced}})：维护工具，包括 [[setting:reload]]、[[setting:clear-saved-data]] 和 [[setting:open-logs]]。
