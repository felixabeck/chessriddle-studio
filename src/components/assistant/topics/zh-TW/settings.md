從應用程式選單開啟 [[page:settings]] ({{label:SideBar.Settings}})（或在 macOS 上按 Cmd+,）。使用 [[page:settings]] 頂端的 {{label:Common.Search}} 列依標題、說明或關鍵字篩選所有設定，按 Escape 清除搜尋。

設定分為九個類別：
- [[settings-category:board]] ({{label:Settings.Board}})：棋盤行為與版面配置，包括 [[setting:piece-dest]]、[[setting:move-highlight]]、[[setting:move-highlight-color]]、[[setting:move-highlight-opacity]]、[[setting:arrows]]、[[setting:variation-arrows]]、[[setting:move-notation]]、[[setting:move-method]]、[[setting:snap-arrows]]、[[setting:consecutive-arrows]]、[[setting:erase-drawables]]、[[setting:auto-promote]]、[[setting:coordinates]]、[[setting:animation-speed]]、[[setting:auto-save]]、[[setting:preview-board]]、[[setting:flip-board-after-move]]、[[setting:scroll-moves]]、[[setting:choose-variation]]、[[setting:material-display]]、[[setting:default-panel-tab]] 與 [[setting:panels-beside-notation]]。
- [[settings-category:inputs]] ({{label:Settings.Inputs}})：透過 [[setting:text-input]] 與 [[setting:spell-check]] 進行文字輸入著法與註解拼字檢查。
- [[settings-category:appearance]] ({{label:Settings.Appearance}})：透過 [[setting:theme]]、[[setting:language]]、{{label:Settings.Appearance.TitleBar}}（適用於 Windows 與 Linux）、[[setting:font-size]]、[[setting:piece-set]]、[[setting:piece-size]]、[[setting:board-image]] 與 [[setting:board-wood-texture]] 進行視覺自訂。
- [[settings-category:sound]] ({{label:Settings.Sound}})：透過 [[setting:volume]] 與 [[setting:sound-collection]] 設定走棋音量與音效包。
- [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}})：自訂鍵盤快捷鍵、使用 {{label:Common.Reset}} 重設快捷鍵，並檢視 {{label:Settings.Drawing}} 滑鼠輔助鍵。
- [[settings-category:directories]] ({{label:Settings.Directories}})：透過 [[setting:files-directory]]、[[setting:databases-directory]]、[[setting:engines-directory]] 與 [[setting:puzzles-directory]] 設定儲存資料夾。
- [[settings-category:repertoire]] ({{label:Settings.Repertoire}})：透過 [[setting:repertoire-depth]]、[[setting:practice-grading]] 與 [[setting:practice-new-per-session]] 設定開局庫覆蓋門檻與練習排程。
- [[settings-category:coach]] ({{label:Settings.AI}})：管理 Coach 與 Assistant 的 AI 連線，並在 [[setting:coach-engine]] 中選擇 Coach 使用的本機引擎。
- [[settings-category:advanced]] ({{label:Settings.Advanced}})：維護工具，包括 [[setting:reload]]、[[setting:clear-saved-data]] 與 [[setting:open-logs]]。
