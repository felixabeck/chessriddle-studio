從應用程式選單開啟 [[page:settings]] ({{label:SideBar.Settings}})（在 macOS 上按 Cmd+,）。使用 [[page:settings]] 頂端的 {{label:Common.Search}} 列依標題、描述、分組或關鍵字篩選所有設定，按 Escape 清空搜尋。

設定分為兩個側邊欄群組：西洋棋與應用程式。

西洋棋：
- [[settings-category:board]] ({{label:Settings.Board}})：棋盤外觀以及如何走子，包括 [[setting:board-image]]、[[setting:board-wood-texture]]、[[setting:piece-set]]、[[setting:piece-size]]、[[setting:coordinates]]、[[setting:animation-speed]]、[[setting:piece-dest]]、[[setting:move-highlight]]、[[setting:move-highlight-color]]、[[setting:move-highlight-opacity]]、[[setting:move-method]]、[[setting:text-input]]、[[setting:auto-promote]]、[[setting:flip-board-after-move]]、[[setting:arrows]]、[[setting:variation-arrows]]、[[setting:snap-arrows]]、[[setting:consecutive-arrows]] 與 [[setting:erase-drawables]]。
- [[settings-category:analysis]] ({{label:Settings.Analysis}})：記譜、導覽、面板配置與自動儲存，透過 [[setting:move-notation]]、[[setting:material-display]]、[[setting:scroll-moves]]、[[setting:choose-variation]]、[[setting:preview-board]]、[[setting:default-panel-tab]]、[[setting:panels-beside-notation]] 與 [[setting:auto-save]]。
- [[settings-category:repertoire]] ({{label:Settings.Repertoire}})：開局庫覆蓋門檻與練習排程，透過 [[setting:repertoire-depth]]、[[setting:practice-grading]] 與 [[setting:practice-new-per-session]]。
- [[settings-category:coach]] ({{label:Settings.AI}})：管理 Coach 與 Assistant 的 AI 連線，在 [[setting:coach-model]] 選擇回答所用模型，並在 [[setting:coach-engine]] 選擇 Coach 的本機引擎。

應用程式：
- [[settings-category:general]] ({{label:Settings.General}})：語言、主題、字級與輸入偏好，透過 [[setting:language]]、[[setting:theme]]、[[setting:font-size]]、{{label:Settings.Appearance.TitleBar}}（適用於 Windows 與 Linux）與 [[setting:spell-check]]。
- [[settings-category:sound]] ({{label:Settings.Sound}})：走子音效音量與音效包，透過 [[setting:volume]] 與 [[setting:sound-collection]]。
- [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}})：自訂鍵盤快捷鍵，用 {{label:Common.Reset}} 重設，並查看 {{label:Settings.Drawing}} 的滑鼠修飾鍵。
- [[settings-category:directories]] ({{label:Settings.Directories}})：透過 [[setting:files-directory]]、[[setting:databases-directory]]、[[setting:engines-directory]] 與 [[setting:puzzles-directory]] 設定儲存資料夾。
- [[settings-category:advanced]] ({{label:Settings.Advanced}})：維護工具，包括 [[setting:reload]]、[[setting:clear-saved-data]] 與 [[setting:open-logs]]。
