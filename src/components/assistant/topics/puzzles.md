id: puzzles
title: Puzzles and tactical training
summary: Solve tactics with ChessRiddle online training or offline puzzle databases.
---

To start solving puzzles, open [[page:new-tab]] and click {{label:Home.Card.Puzzle.Title}}.

When ChessRiddle training is available, you can switch between {{label:Puzzle.Source.ChessRiddle}} and {{label:Puzzle.Source.Offline}} using the source selector at the top of the puzzle panel (or click {{label:Puzzle.Source.UseOffline}} to go straight to offline puzzles).

**{{label:Puzzle.Source.ChessRiddle}}** requires a connected ChessRiddle account (you can connect directly from the puzzle panel or on [[page:accounts]]):
- Switch between {{label:Training.Tactics}} and {{label:Training.Mistakes}} to drill general tactics or revisit missed puzzles.
- Enter moves for both sides on the board, then submit your line.
- Click {{label:Training.Settings.Title}} to choose {{label:Training.Settings.Difficulty}}, {{label:Training.Settings.Motif}}, {{label:Training.Settings.ShowSolutionLength}}, {{label:Training.Settings.ShowTimer}}, and {{label:Training.Settings.TimerMode}}.
- Click {{label:Training.Shortcuts.Title}} (or press `?`) to view training keyboard shortcuts.

**{{label:Puzzle.Source.Offline}}** uses local puzzle databases stored in [[setting:puzzles-directory]]:
- Open the database dropdown and choose {{label:Common.AddNew}} to download a puzzle database from the catalog or click {{label:Puzzle.ChooseLocalFolder}} to pick a local folder.
- Click {{label:SideBar.Settings}} (the gear icon) to set {{label:Puzzle.RatingRange}}, filter by {{label:Puzzle.Theme}}, and toggle {{label:Puzzle.Progressive}}, {{label:Puzzle.HideRating}}, {{label:Puzzle.JumpToNextPuzzleImmediately}}, and {{label:Puzzle.TrackPuzzleTime}}.
- Remove the selected puzzle database with {{label:Puzzle.DeleteDatabase}}.
