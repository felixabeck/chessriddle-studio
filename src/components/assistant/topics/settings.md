Open [[page:settings]] ({{label:SideBar.Settings}}) from the application menu (or press Cmd+, on macOS). Use the {{label:Common.Search}} bar at the top of [[page:settings]] to filter all settings by title, description, group, or keyword, and press Escape to clear the search.

Settings are organized into two sidebar groups, Chess and App:

Chess:
- [[settings-category:board]] ({{label:Settings.Board}}): how the board looks and how you move pieces, including [[setting:board-image]], [[setting:board-wood-texture]], [[setting:piece-set]], [[setting:piece-size]], [[setting:coordinates]], [[setting:animation-speed]], [[setting:piece-dest]], [[setting:move-highlight]], [[setting:move-highlight-color]], [[setting:move-highlight-opacity]], [[setting:move-method]], [[setting:text-input]], [[setting:auto-promote]], [[setting:flip-board-after-move]], [[setting:arrows]], [[setting:variation-arrows]], [[setting:snap-arrows]], [[setting:consecutive-arrows]], and [[setting:erase-drawables]].
- [[settings-category:analysis]] ({{label:Settings.Analysis}}): notation, navigation, panel layout, and auto-save via [[setting:move-notation]], [[setting:material-display]], [[setting:scroll-moves]], [[setting:choose-variation]], [[setting:preview-board]], [[setting:default-panel-tab]], [[setting:panels-beside-notation]], and [[setting:auto-save]].
- [[settings-category:repertoire]] ({{label:Settings.Repertoire}}): repertoire coverage threshold and practice scheduling via [[setting:repertoire-depth]], [[setting:practice-grading]], and [[setting:practice-new-per-session]].
- [[settings-category:coach]] ({{label:Settings.AI}}): manage AI connections for the Coach and Assistant, choose the model they answer with in [[setting:coach-model]], and choose the Coach's local engine in [[setting:coach-engine]].

App:
- [[settings-category:general]] ({{label:Settings.General}}): language, theme, font size, and input preferences via [[setting:language]], [[setting:theme]], [[setting:font-size]], {{label:Settings.Appearance.TitleBar}} (on Windows and Linux), and [[setting:spell-check]].
- [[settings-category:sound]] ({{label:Settings.Sound}}): move audio volume and sound pack via [[setting:volume]] and [[setting:sound-collection]].
- [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}}): customize keyboard shortcuts, reset them with {{label:Common.Reset}}, and view the {{label:Settings.Drawing}} mouse modifiers.
- [[settings-category:directories]] ({{label:Settings.Directories}}): configure storage folders via [[setting:files-directory]], [[setting:databases-directory]], [[setting:engines-directory]], and [[setting:puzzles-directory]].
- [[settings-category:advanced]] ({{label:Settings.Advanced}}): maintenance tools including [[setting:reload]], [[setting:clear-saved-data]], and [[setting:open-logs]].
