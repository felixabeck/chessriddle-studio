title: Online accounts and opponent preparation
summary: Add Lichess and Chess.com accounts, download your games, and link online accounts to database players to study an opponent's openings.
---

Manage your Lichess and Chess.com accounts on [[page:accounts]] ({{label:SideBar.Accounts}}).

To add an account:
1. Click {{label:Home.Accounts.Add}} on [[page:accounts]].
2. Optionally enter or select a {{label:Home.Accounts.PlayerName}} to group multiple accounts under one player profile.
3. Choose Lichess or Chess.com under {{label:Home.Accounts.Website}} and enter the {{label:Home.Accounts.Username}}.
4. For Lichess, optionally check {{label:Home.Accounts.LoginWithBrowser}} to sign in with OAuth. Signing in unlocks faster game downloads and the Lichess online opening explorers on the analysis board.
5. Click {{label:Common.Add}}.

On each account card on [[page:accounts]]:
- Click {{label:Home.Accounts.UpdateStats}} to refresh your ratings and stats.
- Click the download button to download your games into a local database (subsequent downloads fetch only new games since the last download).
- Click {{label:Home.Accounts.RemoveAccount}} to remove the account.
- Once games are downloaded, the right side of [[page:accounts]] displays your personal statistics ({{label:Home.Personal.Overview}}, {{label:Home.Personal.Ratings}}, and {{label:Home.Personal.Openings}}).

To study an opponent's online openings from a database:
1. Open a database in [[page:library]] and switch to the {{label:Databases.Card.Players}} tab.
2. Select a player to open their player card.
3. Click {{label:Databases.Opponent.Add}} to link one or more Lichess or Chess.com usernames to that player.
4. Click {{label:Databases.Opponent.Manage}} to filter by {{label:Databases.OpeningTree.Since}} and {{label:Databases.Opponent.TimeControls}}, or to {{label:Databases.Opponent.RemoveGames}} or {{label:Databases.Opponent.Unlink}}.
5. Click {{label:Databases.Opponent.Download}} (or {{label:Databases.Opponent.Update}}) to fetch their online games.
6. Open the {{label:Home.Personal.Openings}} tab on the player card to browse their over-the-board and online opening tree, filter by color and {{label:Databases.OpeningTree.Since}}, and click {{label:Databases.OpeningTree.Prepare}} to open the line on an analysis board with the opponent's games pinned in the explorer.
