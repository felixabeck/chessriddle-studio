Alle Tastenkürzel kannst du unter [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}}) auf [[page:settings]] einsehen und anpassen. Die Kürzel stehen in betitelten Gruppen. Klicke auf ein Kürzel in einer Gruppe, um eine neue Tastenkombination aufzunehmen, oder klicke auf {{label:Common.Reset}} neben der Überschrift, um alle Standardwerte wiederherzustellen.

Häufige Standardkürzel (Strg unter Windows/Linux und Cmd unter macOS):
- Tabs und Dateien: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEW_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+T`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLOSE_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+W`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.OPEN_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+O`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SAVE_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+S`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SAVE_TO_DATABASE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+D`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.COPY_PGN}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+K`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Tab`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REVERSE_CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Shift+Tab`).
- Navigation: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_MOVE}} (`Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_MOVE}} (`Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_START}} (`Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_END}} (`Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_START}} (`Shift+Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_END}} (`Shift+Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCH}} (`C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCH}} (`Shift+C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_OPEN_VARIATION}} (`U`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_GAME}} (`PageUp`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_GAME}} (`PageDown`).
- Brett und Bearbeitung: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SWAP_ORIENTATION}} (`F`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.EDIT_POSITION}} (`E`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TAKE_SNAPSHOT}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+S`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.UNDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_MOVE}} (`Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_VARIATION}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_MOVES_AFTER}} (`Shift+Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PROMOTE_VARIATION}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLEAR_SHAPES}} (`Ctrl+L`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_BLUR}} (`Ctrl+B`).
- Analyse und Panels: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_EVAL_BAR}} (`Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_ALL_ENGINES}} (`Ctrl+A`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PLAY_BEST_ENGINE_MOVE}} (`Space`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_THREAT_MODE}} (`X`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GENERATE_REPORT}} (`F12`), sowie die Panel-Tasten `P` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Practice}}), `A` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Analysis}}), `B` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Database}}), `D` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Annotate}}), `O` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Coach}}) und `I` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Info}}).
- Annotationen: `1` bis `6` schalten `!!`, `!`, `!?`, `?!`, `?` und `??` um. Im Kommentareditor springst du mit `Tab` und `Shift+Tab` zum nächsten oder vorherigen Zug.
- Vollbild: `F11` unter Windows/Linux, `Ctrl+Cmd+F` unter macOS.
- Fokusmodus: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_FOCUS_MODE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+F`) blendet die Kopfzeile mit den Tabs und Fensterknöpfen aus. Bewege den Mauszeiger an den oberen Fensterrand, um sie kurz wieder einzublenden.

Ganz unten unter [[settings-category:keybinds]] stehen außerdem die Maus-Modifikatoren für {{label:Settings.Drawing}} zum Zeichnen farbiger Pfeile und Kreise auf dem Brett.
