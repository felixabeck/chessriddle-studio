Visualizza e personalizza tutti i tasti di scelta rapida in [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}}) su [[page:settings]]. Fai clic su qualsiasi scorciatoia nella tabella per registrare una nuova combinazione di tasti, oppure fai clic su {{label:Common.Reset}} accanto al titolo per ripristinare tutti i valori predefiniti.

Scorciatoie predefinite comuni (con Ctrl su Windows/Linux e Cmd su macOS):
- Schede e file: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEW_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+T`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLOSE_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+W`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.OPEN_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+O`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SAVE_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+S`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.COPY_PGN}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+K`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Tab`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REVERSE_CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Shift+Tab`).
- Navigazione: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_MOVE}} (`Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_MOVE}} (`Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_START}} (`Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_END}} (`Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_START}} (`Shift+Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_END}} (`Shift+Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCH}} (`C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCH}} (`Shift+C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_OPEN_VARIATION}} (`U`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_GAME}} (`PageUp`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_GAME}} (`PageDown`).
- Scacchiera e modifica: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SWAP_ORIENTATION}} (`F`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.UNDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_MOVE}} (`Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLEAR_SHAPES}} (`Ctrl+L`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_BLUR}} (`Ctrl+B`).
- Analisi e pannelli: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_EVAL_BAR}} (`Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_ALL_ENGINES}} (`Ctrl+A`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PLAY_BEST_ENGINE_MOVE}} (`Space`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_THREAT_MODE}} (`X`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GENERATE_REPORT}} (`F12`), oltre ai tasti pannello `P` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Practice}}), `A` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Analysis}}), `B` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Database}}), `D` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Annotate}}), `O` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Coach}}) e `I` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Info}}).
- Annotazioni: i tasti da `1` a `6` attivano o disattivano `!!`, `!`, `!?`, `?!`, `?` e `??`. Dentro l’editor dei commenti, `Tab` e `Shift+Tab` passano alla mossa successiva o precedente.
- Schermo intero: `F11` su Windows/Linux, `Ctrl+Cmd+F` su macOS.

In fondo a [[settings-category:keybinds]] trovi anche i modificatori del mouse per {{label:Settings.Drawing}} per tracciare frecce e cerchi colorati sulla scacchiera.
