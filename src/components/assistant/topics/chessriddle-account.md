---

Your ChessRiddle account connects Studio to {{label:Puzzle.Source.ChessRiddle}} online puzzle training.

Manage the connection in the {{label:Home.ChessRiddle.Title}} card on [[page:accounts]] ({{label:SideBar.Accounts}}), or directly from the puzzle panel when opening {{label:Home.Card.Puzzle.Title}} on [[page:new-tab]].

- Click {{label:Home.ChessRiddle.Connect}} to sign in through your browser. Your session credentials are stored in your system keyring.
- Once connected, the card shows your username and plan (Free or Gold).
- Click {{label:Home.ChessRiddle.Refresh}} to check your current account status and plan.
- If your session expires, click {{label:Home.ChessRiddle.Reconnect}} to sign in again.
- Click {{label:Home.ChessRiddle.Disconnect}} to remove the account from this computer and sign out.
- Once connected, click {{label:Library.Sync.Action}} on [[page:library]] to sync selected PGN files and game databases to your private ChessRiddle Library (one-way, read-only on the website).
