Consulta y personaliza todos los atajos de teclado en [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}}) de [[page:settings]]. Los atajos aparecen en grupos con título. Haz clic en cualquier atajo de un grupo para grabar una nueva combinación, o pulsa {{label:Common.Reset}} junto al encabezado para restaurar todos los valores predeterminados.

Atajos predeterminados habituales (con Ctrl en Windows/Linux y Cmd en macOS):
- Pestañas y archivos: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEW_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+T`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLOSE_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+W`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.OPEN_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+O`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SAVE_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+S`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.COPY_PGN}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+K`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Tab`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REVERSE_CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Shift+Tab`).
- Navegación: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_MOVE}} (`Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_MOVE}} (`Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_START}} (`Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_END}} (`Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_START}} (`Shift+Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_END}} (`Shift+Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCH}} (`C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCH}} (`Shift+C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_OPEN_VARIATION}} (`U`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_GAME}} (`PageUp`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_GAME}} (`PageDown`).
- Tablero y edición: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SWAP_ORIENTATION}} (`F`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.UNDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_MOVE}} (`Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLEAR_SHAPES}} (`Ctrl+L`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_BLUR}} (`Ctrl+B`).
- Análisis y paneles: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_EVAL_BAR}} (`Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_ALL_ENGINES}} (`Ctrl+A`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PLAY_BEST_ENGINE_MOVE}} (`Space`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_THREAT_MODE}} (`X`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GENERATE_REPORT}} (`F12`), además de las teclas de panel `P` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Practice}}), `A` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Analysis}}), `B` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Database}}), `D` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Annotate}}), `O` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Coach}}) e `I` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Info}}).
- Anotaciones: del `1` al `6` alternan `!!`, `!`, `!?`, `?!`, `?` y `??`. Dentro del editor de comentarios, `Tab` y `Shift+Tab` pasan a la jugada siguiente o anterior.
- Pantalla completa: `F11` en Windows/Linux, `Ctrl+Cmd+F` en macOS.

La parte inferior de [[settings-category:keybinds]] también enumera los modificadores de ratón de {{label:Settings.Drawing}} para dibujar flechas y círculos de colores en el tablero.
