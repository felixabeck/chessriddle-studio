title: Tabs and the New tab page
summary: Open, switch, reorder, rename, duplicate, and close tabs, and start work from the New tab page.
---

The navigation row at the top holds the application menu, a pinned link to [[page:library]] ({{label:SideBar.Library}}), any open page tabs ([[page:accounts]], [[page:engines]], [[page:settings]]), your board tabs, and the {{label:Navigation.Feedback}} and {{label:Navigation.Assistant}} buttons.

To open a new tab, click {{label:Tab.NewTab}} (the plus button in the tab strip), use {{label:Menu.File}} -> {{label:Menu.File.NewTab}}, or press the {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEW_TAB}} shortcut. You can also jump to [[page:new-tab]].

On [[page:new-tab]], the action buttons start a workflow in the current tab:
- {{label:Home.Card.PlayChess.Button}}: play against an engine or another human.
- {{label:Home.Card.AnalysisBoard.Title}}: open an empty analysis board.
- {{label:Home.Card.Puzzle.Title}}: open puzzle training.
- {{label:Home.Card.NewRepertoire.Title}}: create a new opening repertoire file.
- {{label:Home.Card.ImportGame.Button}}: import a PGN file, pasted PGN, online game link, or FEN.

Below the actions, [[page:new-tab]] lists your library and recent games. Use the {{label:Home.Library.Search}} box and the {{label:Home.Library.All}}, {{label:Home.Library.Repertoires}}, {{label:Home.Library.Games}}, and {{label:Home.Library.Due}} filters to find items, or click {{label:Home.Library.OpenLibrary}} to go to [[page:library]].

Manage board tabs from the tab strip:
- Drag a tab to reorder it.
- Right-click a tab (or press Shift+F10 on a focused tab) to {{label:Tab.Duplicate}}, {{label:Tab.Rename}}, or {{label:Tab.Close}}. You can also press F2 on a focused tab to rename it.
- When many tabs are open, click {{label:Tab.AllTabs}} to pick any tab from a list.
- Close a page tab ([[page:accounts]], [[page:engines]], [[page:settings]]) using its close button.
