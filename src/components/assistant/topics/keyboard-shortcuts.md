View and customize all keyboard shortcuts in [[settings-category:keybinds]] ({{label:Settings.Keybinds}}) on [[page:settings]]. Shortcuts are listed in titled groups. Click any shortcut in a group to record a new key combination, or click {{label:Common.Reset}} beside the heading to restore all defaults.

Common default shortcuts (using Ctrl on Windows/Linux and Cmd on macOS):
- Tabs and files: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEW_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+T`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEW_ANALYSIS}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+N`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLOSE_TAB}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+W`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.OPEN_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+O`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SAVE_FILE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+S`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SAVE_TO_DATABASE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+D`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.COPY_PGN}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+K`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Tab`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REVERSE_CYCLE_TABS}} (`Ctrl+Shift+Tab`).
- Navigation: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_MOVE}} (`Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_MOVE}} (`Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_START}} (`Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_BRANCH_END}} (`Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_START}} (`Shift+Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GO_TO_END}} (`Shift+Down`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCH}} (`C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCH}} (`Shift+C`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Right`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_BRANCHING}} (`Shift+Left`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_OPEN_VARIATION}} (`U`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PREVIOUS_GAME}} (`PageUp`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.NEXT_GAME}} (`PageDown`).
- Board and editing: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.SWAP_ORIENTATION}} (`F`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.EDIT_POSITION}} (`E`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TAKE_SNAPSHOT}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+S`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.UNDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.REDO}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_MOVE}} (`Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_VARIATION}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.DELETE_MOVES_AFTER}} (`Shift+Delete`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PROMOTE_VARIATION}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Up`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.CLEAR_SHAPES}} (`Ctrl+L`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_BLUR}} (`Ctrl+B`).
- Analysis and panels: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_EVAL_BAR}} (`Z`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_ALL_ENGINES}} (`Ctrl+A`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.PLAY_BEST_ENGINE_MOVE}} (`Space`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_THREAT_MODE}} (`X`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.GENERATE_REPORT}} (`F12`), panel keys `P` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Practice}}), `A` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Analysis}}), `B` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Database}}), `D` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Annotate}}), `O` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Coach}}), and `I` ({{label:Board.Tabs.Info}}), and layout shortcuts {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.APPLY_LAYOUT_1}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+1`), {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.APPLY_LAYOUT_2}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+2`), and {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.APPLY_LAYOUT_3}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Alt+3`).
- Annotations: `1` through `6` toggle `!!`, `!`, `!?`, `?!`, `?`, and `??`. Inside the comment editor, `Tab` and `Shift+Tab` move to the next or previous move.
- Fullscreen: `F11` on Windows/Linux, `Ctrl+Cmd+F` on macOS.
- Focus mode: {{label:Settings.Keybinds.Actions.TOGGLE_FOCUS_MODE}} (`Ctrl/Cmd+Shift+F`) hides the header with the tabs and window buttons. Move the pointer to the top edge of the window to show it again for a moment.

The bottom of [[settings-category:keybinds]] also lists the {{label:Settings.Drawing}} mouse modifiers for drawing colored arrows and circles on the board.
